## Launch post

I’ve been building FUDI-DC, a math learning workspace designed around a simple idea: students should be able to see a question and work through it on the same page.

The web app brings guided practice together with a drawable notebook, with question-linked pages, hints, worked explanations, and progress saved locally. Its curated question bank ranges from algebra and geometry through calculus, linear algebra, statistics, discrete math, and differential equations.

I’m also building the next layer: randomized questions from the WeBWorK Open Problem Library and experimental notebook feedback that runs on Apple Silicon using a local vision model. The work is being shaped around keeping notes on the learner’s device and making any cloud upload an explicit choice.

FUDI-DC is a noncommercial portfolio project, and AI feedback is experimental. I’m continuing to test the learning flow, accessibility, and privacy boundaries as the product grows.

Building this has brought together product design, front-end engineering, math content, local data storage, and native Mac development. I’m excited to keep improving it.
