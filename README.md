# FUDI-DC

FUDI-DC is an AI-powered mathematics learning and practice platform designed to support learners from foundational calculus through multivariable calculus and into related advanced mathematics, including linear algebra and other connected prerequisite topics.

## Learn, practise, and improve

- **Daily problem practice:** Generate a varied selection of mathematical problems across topics and difficulty levels.
- **Notebook-based learning:** Learn concepts and work through problems in a dedicated notebook.
- **AI voice explanations:** Follow spoken explanations of mathematical topics alongside the notebook.
- **Performance overview:** Review how you performed, identify areas that need more work, and track your progress.

FUDI-DC aims to bring explanations, problem-solving, and feedback together in one learning experience, helping learners build the mathematical foundations they need as they progress to more advanced topics.
